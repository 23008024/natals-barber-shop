Privacy Policy

Information about the personal information we collect, why we use it and how you can contact us.

Last updated: 24 September 2026

1. Introduction

Natal's Barber Shop respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, contact us or submit an appointment request.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number
  • Your selected barber and service
  • Your requested appointment date and time
  • Additional information included in your message or booking notes

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our booking and contact forms may be used to:

  • Process appointment requests
  • Respond to contact enquiries
  • Communicate with you about an appointment
  • Provide information about our services
  • Maintain appropriate booking and customer records

4. Online Forms

Our website uses online form services to transmit information submitted through booking and contact forms. Information submitted through these forms may be processed by the relevant third-party form provider in accordance with its own privacy practices.

Customers should avoid submitting sensitive personal information that is not necessary for an appointment or enquiry.

5. Email Communication

Contact and booking information may be sent to Natal's Barber Shop by email so that appointment requests and enquiries can be managed.

We use the information provided for the purpose for which it was submitted and to communicate with customers about their requests.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

The website may use browser-based storage for certain functionality, such as remembering information related to a booking confirmation.

You can manage or clear browser storage through your browser settings.

7. Third-Party Services

Some website features may rely on third-party services, such as form processing, maps or calendar functionality. These services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website. However, no method of transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Choices

You may contact Natal's Barber Shop if you have questions about information you have submitted through our website or if you would like to request clarification about how your information is used.

10. Children's Information

Our services include children's haircuts, but bookings for children should be made by a parent or responsible adult. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children through this website.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. The latest version will be published on this page with the relevant update date.
